About

Hi, I’m Pratik Akkawar — Welcome to The Simplified Life!

I believe that a simpler life is a more meaningful life. The Simplified Life is a space where I share my journey towards creating more room for what truly matters — whether it’s time, focus, or peace of mind.

My goal is to help you:

  • Cut through the noise of modern life and focus on what you value
  • Streamline your daily routines to reduce stress
  • Make intentional choices that align with your goals
  • Create balance in your work, relationships, and personal growth

This isn’t about perfection; it’s about making space for more of what makes you feel alive. If you’re looking for a more thoughtful, focused way to live, you’re in the right place.

Let’s explore The Simplified Life together!
