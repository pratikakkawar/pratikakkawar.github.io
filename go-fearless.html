Go fearless

Pratik Akkawar Reflections

When you live with the fear in your mind, you minimize the number of possibilities. Life is about taking chances whenever and wherever possible. You don't because you get scare and feel the fear before anything could happen. Most of the time you change your decisions just because you feel afraid of the future happenings. The fear of failure is most likely to hit us while taking any decision. At the time of taking decisions we don't really count the possibilities and fear is the only reason behind it. Sometimes, the feeling of fear gets so strong that we decide to quit on certain things even when we wanted to go for it. It is the fear which holds you back from time to time.

If you see the world around you carefully, you'll find that people who are living their dream life are the people who have managed to overcome their fears. It is not that we feel afraid of only big things, sometimes it could be as small as giving smile. The fear of acceptance keeps us away from wonderful people, the fear of negligence hold us back from being part of beautiful relations.

You will witness a completely different life when you'll overcome these small (but big enough to hold you back) fears in your life.

Live more, fear less. GO FEARLESS.
