Awkward eye contact

Pratik Akkawar June 10, 2015 Stories

He gets to pass by her only once a day and he look at her every time he pass by her. She with curiosity to cross check whether he's looking at her or not takes her eyes off the screen placed in front of her to look at him and ends up making an awkward eye contact. That awkward eye contact between them is making him crazy and he's afraid that he's falling, in love with her.
