Never ending weeks of waiting

Pratik Akkawar October 1, 2015 Stories

Miracles don’t happen every day! Not that, he didn’t know that but still he was expecting another one to happen. A week later, he found himself checking his phone more often than he used to be. Since last seven days, he had visited her profile hundreds of times. His social media search history would show a long list of recent searches made, having her name on every single row of it. Why was he doing all that? He had no clues; maybe he had become desperate to reach her, talk to her. He very well knew that she was not going to respond very soon as he already had seen her frequency of being active on the social media. She would use that once in the time span of two to three months. He had roughly calculated the probability of she accessing her profile again and there was a very less possibility that she would and get to see a pending friend request from him.

He was experiencing the reality. In the process of looking for an alternate and comparatively easy way to reach her, he had put himself in an infinite loop of waiting. On normal days, he would hate to wait for anything to happen but in this case, he was helpless. He had no options left for him except one, dare to walk to her and talk, in front of the office staff which would not care to look at them even if he do so.

He had waited for months to find the right time and in the process he had trapped himself in never-ending weeks of waiting.
