The story of 8 chamans

Those were the best days of my life !

Year 2009, 8 friends, 1 destination - Dugarwadi Full of adventure, full of fun, full of rain, full of water 4 bikes, muddy roads, water puddles, dirty motor wheels 1 house, no parking slot, 2 bikes chained together

beautiful waterfall, awesome weather, stunning photo shoot green trees, green grass, single way to the core high water level, horrible river flow, man made chain hand in hand, crossing two sides, enjoying in the rain

jumps in water, wet sand game, wet clothes, wet hairs triple jump in water, back pain, traveling with wave tall standing waterfall, water everywhere, cool fresh air swimming, dancing, yelling, shower in rain, green snake

the joy, the happiness, the pleasure, the satisfaction the lost keys, long search in water, the little fear on faces words to comfort, extra fear, too much laughter with stress tired legs, tired body, tired minds, cheerful hearts

wet bags, water in mobiles, more laughter again 1 stall, full packed tiffins, hot bhaji, steams from cup of tea a call to a friend, unnecessary traveled distance unbroken chain, broken lock, small kid to help

back to home, unforgettable trip, touching memories
