Happy New Year – In Progress

Pratik Akkawar Moments

Writing has never been a best part of me. I can’t write as fast as I think, imagine and visualize things in my head. There are people who synchronize these very well. I lack this quality for sure. Anyways, I am trying my best to keep things moving and today I’m glad to announce that there’s some work in progress. Basically, it will be a short story but more than that it will convey some message for sure. In fact, I first composed the script accordingly and then decided to write it down on paper (Well, on word file I guess).

So, why a short story? I guess there was no better option to convey my message than to go for short story. I could have gone for visual stuff but I’m not good at that. However, I will launch a short teaser of it in video format for all of you hopefully before 20th November. The story is titled as “Happy New Year”. I hope you will like it and have fun while reading.

P.S. – Short stories don’t take much time to get read completely. Considering this, you should be thanking writers who write short stuff (Just kidding). Just want to say must read when it will be out.
