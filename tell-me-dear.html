Tell me dear

Can your heart skip a beat merely upon listening to their voice? Is it possible to touch someone’s heart without touching their body? Can a single thought of someone whom you haven’t even met bring the smile on your face? Can you fall in love with someone who is thousands of miles away? Tell me dear.

They say love is blind. They say love has no barriers. But, is it love? This anonymous feeling I get, is it love? I don’t know.

All I know is, the day we met over the internet was one of the best days of my life and I was so much fortunate to find you from among the billions of people in the world. With your presence, you brought the immense joy to my life which was missing since the ages. In you I found the best friend, with whom I can share the secrets and talk nonsense. You inspired and motivated me. You brought the positivity in me. You taught me to dream, dream big and impossible. You taught me to believe in myself. You enlighten the hope in me, once again. You showed me the way. You illuminated my path. You held my hand. You became my companion.

In you, I see a dreamer, who dreams of the wonderful world. In you, I see a believer, who believes that dreams can be turned into reality. In you, I see a fighter, fighting against the world  to make dreams reality. With your achievements, you inspire their souls. With your grace, you touch their hearts. You are someone, anyone would fall for. Am I falling for you? Tell me dear.

I lose my all senses as soon as I listen to you over the phone. I smile, every time I get a text from you. My eyes sparkle when I see you in pictures. I feel the bliss, when I see you happy. I feel the sorrow, when you sound sad. I can feel what you feel. I can sense the emotions you go through. I can read your mind. I care for you. I worry for you. I feel alone, when you don’t talk. I see you in my dreams, dreaming along with me. I now dream of the dreams wherein we are together. I wish to be with you in the dark and bright. I wish to be with you in good and bad times. I wish to be in your heart. I wish to be your soul-mate. For this moment, for this lifetime!

But, do the feelings travel through distance? Do the magic, called love work miles away?

Have you ever felt it? Have you felt it the same way? Tell me dear.
