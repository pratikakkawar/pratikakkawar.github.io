Moments of celebration

Pratik Akkawar October 7, 2015 Interests

Type of photography – General

Place – At home, Nashik

Date – 30th September 2015

Time – Late Evening

Background – It's fine, I said when they suggested to celebrate my birthday later on weekend. I too wasn't very keen to cut the cake and get my special day celebrated at the very moment. However, my bro insisted to have celebration right away and bought the cake for me, late in the evening. After that, no one was stopping us. We wore new dresses, had family photo-shoot and I finally cut the cake. We enjoyed every moment of it. Hadn't my bro insisted, we would have missed all the fun. I eventually realized the importance of celebration in our day-to-day life.

A message – Life is too short to miss on celebrations. Each moment is precious and the ones belonging to special occasions deserved to be celebrated. One should celebrate each special event for that brings smiles on faces and spreads love among hearts. Celebrations make it all precious and priceless. Go, celebrate and have fun. :D
