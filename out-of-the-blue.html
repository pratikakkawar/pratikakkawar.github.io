Out of the blue

I still don’t know why he had called me up so early in the morning, I still don’t know whether it was planned before or happened in the mean while but one thing was sure that it came out of the blue for me. I thought it would be a casual hang out we guys usually have or we might be talking about his new journey or we might be discussing something serious about happenings in his life but to the surprise it came out to be a Birthday Celebration. More than just a birthday it seemed like celebration of friendship. Thank you so much guys for putting so much into our still rocking journey and I hope we will continue in same way in future too.

Besides that, I just want to mention here that the cake was really sweet and nice as like our Group. The group photograph is awesome and is still lightning into my eyes. Yeah, your Smart phone is really smart and have really good quality camera. No doubts buddy. Moreover, one thing you guys must have to admit is that the end would not have been so hilarious if Police Van did not make it to the spot. I am glad though as we have decent history of amazing happenings during any Thaww Event and the show is still going on. You guys rocked, however I missed a couple of them. Nevertheless, I had great fun and looking forward to new adventures and happening with all of us.

showstopper: Remember those anonymous guys shaking our hands and wishing us happy birthday? Yeah, for me they were the real showstoppers of the event. Moreover, one of them came up just to frame all of us in one picture. Thank you so much buddy, you have a big and kind heart. May god bless you.

P.S.: All the references like he/his/him are kept intentionally and I hope they will be guessed correctly by Thawwers.
