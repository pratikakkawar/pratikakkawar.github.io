Like And Share The Quotes

pratikakkawar April 29, 2013

Nishabd is continuously working hard to make it's visitor's experience better. So far it had facility to like, share and comment on poems, unfortunately Quotes did not have that facility then. Today, we are glad to announce that, now you can like or share the Quotes also from nishabd.com. You can tweet the Quotes as well.

There are much more improvements remained to make and we are still working on it. Any suggestions about improvements of the website are welcome and appreciated. Suggestions regarding our blog are also welcome. We promise to try our best to bring you the best of everything.
