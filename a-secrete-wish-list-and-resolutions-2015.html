A secrete wish list and resolutions 2015

As we all know it’s the very first day of brand new year 2015 and it is not just like any other day. The first day of a new year holds lots of importance in a way that it is considered as the new beginning. It is the new beginning for most of us; it brings new hope, belief and inspiration to our lives. I too don’t want to miss this opportunity so wish you a very happy new year guys. I just wish you health, wealth and piece in the upcoming year. May all your dreams and wishes come true and happiness keep knocking your door throughout the year.

Well,we all know that with the new year comes new resolutions. Yeah, I know many of you won’t be making any as you think you won’t make it or it is just a madness or show off but I had started to set resolutions from the last year. I even had shared my New Year Resolutions for year 2014 on blog one year ago. Yeah, the excitement of setting new ones has taken on looking back and take review of old ones but yes, I will be writing a review of my resolutions 2014 soon so wait a little for that.

With new learning and new experiences you tend to become a new person. Over the year I learned and experienced great things. This year I have set my resolutions by taking into account what I really want to do or achieve or become in my life. I won’t be sharing what exactly I mean with the last lines but yes I will try to explain each and every resolution set for this year. Well, I have carried couple of them from my last year, no, they are not same and you will find out why so go ahead and read them out.

To exercise for at least 6 months –

Last year I had set this one as optional but this year doing daily workout will be one of the important thing in my list. I just hope you won’t expect reason for this one as we all know how important it is to stay healthy.

Reading at least 5 story books –

Yes, this is also carried out from my last year’s list but this time I have set some criteria. I will be reading at least one book from local language, one based on inspiration and one with foreign author. Reading is as important as eating. Reading is just like food to our brain. More you read more you develop as person.

Some social work –

I don’t know whether 2015 will bring me any good or it will serve me worst but I will try to bring few beautiful moments in lives of needy. There are people who need us to help them survive and I would love to play my small part by doing any kind of social work.

Launch my dream website –

I always wanted to launch a website of my own which will spread love and happiness in the whole world. I want to show direction and ways to live a better life. Just to let you know that I am currently working on the website and just hope that I will launch it pretty soon this year.

Learn Spanish language –

I got fascinated with numbers when I researched; the number of languages spoken in the whole world is just incredible. I have decided to add one more extra language in my account as it will help me increase my knowledge plus help me grow as a person as well. I will share why I chose Spanish only later but for now you can help me to start with by sharing fundamental information about Spanish. You can contact me or befriend me on social media. I would love to learn from you.

Click and capture at least 50 photographs –

I have never been a good photographer. In fact, I have very less interest in capturing moments into camera. I like to see photographs and I enjoy them being captured by others but don’t know I just step back when it comes to take a picture. This year, I want to change this story so I have decided that I will capture at least 50 photographs and share them on my blog as a proof. I will be adding one more category for same very soon on my blog. Don’t miss to see and yes any tips and suggestions are welcome.

Well, besides these resolutions, there will be another great thing I will be doing from this year. Yeah, I have made a secrete wish list. No, I’m not going to share the list here. It is a secrete, so just don’t ask anything about that (here). ;)

What are your resolutions for this year? Had you any in the past? Did you achieve them? Share whatever you want relating to Resolutions here. I would like to listen to you.

Hope you guys have a great year coming your way.

Cheers and Happy New Year.
