Lamp of hope

Type of photography – Object

Place – Home

Date – 29th Mar 2015

Time – Late Evening

Background – A surprised electricity cut and the whole world entered into darkness. I was unable to see anything and struggled to find my cell phone however my mom successfully found her old Light Lamp with the help of little Diya. As she enlighten the lamp, the world turned into brightness and I could see that a small flame is enough to overcome the darkness.

A message – That willingness, that eagerness to be on the brighter side matters a lot. And, the efforts taken to be on brighter side are worth of everything. Always believe that single ray hope is enough to face any difficulty in life.
