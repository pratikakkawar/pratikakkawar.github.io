Miracle or Destiny?

Pratik Akkawar Stories

She had disappeared all of a sudden. He looked for her for next few days but she didn’t show up. His eyes searched for hers even in her absence. He was losing hopes bit by bit. Few more days like that and he was convinced about the end of the chapter. It wasn’t a big deal for him but still he missed, missed those eyes very badly through days and nights. On contrary, he would not wish to see her again rather accept the fact that he won’t get to.

One fine day, he was informed by his friend. She had returned. He went on to be on cloud nine but still reacted calmly. He could not believe and remained unconvinced until he saw her again, after a long one and half month. She was there, seated at her place, looking fresh & beautiful as she used to be. He smiled to himself. ‘Miracle or Destiny?’ he thought and wondered.
