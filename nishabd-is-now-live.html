Nishabd Is Now Live

pratikakkawar 9 Apr 2013

A warm welcome to all ! (As it’s summer going on here). I am very excited and happy to announce that nishabd.com is almost ready to go and it will be up and running from April 11, 2013.

There is another big occassion on this particular day. It’s Gudhi padava on April 11, 2013. It is the first day of the Hindu calendar month ‘Chaitra’ and is celebrated as New Year's Day for Maharashtrians.

It will be the new beginning for me and so for the Nishabd. I hope this year brings new hope and happiness to everyone's life. May all your dreams and wishes come true this year.  And I hope Nishabd will also make it’s good beginning from here. So, wish you all a very happy new year. Enjoy and have fun. Stay connected as Nishabd is now alive.
