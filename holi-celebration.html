Holi celebration

Pratik Akkawar Interests

Type of photography – Festive/Celebration

Place – Outside my home

Date – 5th Mar 2015

Time – Night

Background – I was away from home enjoying my time with best buddies. I was aware of the fact that I would not get to see the Holi Bonfire back at my home. Being aware of the situation I returned to home with no hope of witnessing the beautiful Holi but to my surprise it was still on fire even after the long time. I had thought that I was too late to reach the place but as they say the Holi itself symbioses the victory of good over evil and with me it was victory of optimism over  pessimism even though I was on the wrong side this time. Overall, it taught the lesson of never losing hope in anything. I enjoyed the time later and found my #3 photograph.

A message – Never loose hope as it has the power to make the miracles happen. Be hopeful and see the difference. I wish every one out there a very happy Holi. Take time out from your busy life to celebrate the little moments that brings the satisfaction and happiness which will last forever.
